# Deterministic Shadbala, relationship geometry, Jaimini and Bhava Bala engine modules

This change retires LLM agents 1B/1C/1D by adding five deterministic compute modules — `shadbala.ts`, `relationships.ts`, `nakshatraRelationships.ts`, `jaimini.ts`, `bhavaBala.ts` — plus the D2/D3/D12 divisional charts and a Vimshottari sub-lord that the new modules depend on. Step 14 of `computeFullChart` wires them into `ComputedChart`. The constants and formulas track the spec's "REQUIREMENTS CORRECTIONS ADDENDUM" (FIX-1…FIX-13) closely: FIX-1 dignity values, FIX-2 Naisargika virupas, FIX-3 two-part Ojha-Yugma, FIX-4 binary Drekkana with Saturn neuter, FIX-5 rupa units, FIX-6 proportional Natonnata, FIX-8 Shadvarga Vimsopaka weights and FIX-9 graded Drik aspects are all faithfully implemented, and the exaltation longitudes and D2/D3/D12 sign maps are correct. `tsc --noEmit` passes.

**Watch for:** confirmed — Sun's Cheshta is hardcoded to a flat 30, directly contradicting FIX-7 ("Sun's Cheshta = its Ayana Bala, *not* flat 30"); Ayana Bala is implemented nowhere, so Sun's total Shadbala and Ishta/Kashta are off-spec. confirmed — the Saptavargaja compound-relationship map can never yield the Sama (7.5) grade that FIX-1 lists, collapsing five levels into four and mis-scoring perm-friend/temp-enemy and perm-enemy/temp-friend placements. confirmed — Rahu/Ketu cast full 7th graded aspects inside Drik Bala and Bhava Drishti, which classical Shadbala excludes. confirmed — combust benefics are demoted to malefic in `computeDrikBala` but *not* in `computeBhavaBala`'s drishti sum, so the two aspect tallies disagree. confirmed — Vara/Hora/Abda use the civil `getDay()`/date rather than the sunrise-to-sunrise Vedic day, giving pre-sunrise births the wrong weekday lord.

## High-level view

The six-fold Shadbala is assembled per planet from correctly-separated sub-components, and the arithmetic units line up: everything sums in virupas, `totalRupas = totalVirupas/60`, and `strengthRatio = totalRupas/requiredRupas` against the FIX-5 rupa minimums. The corrected constant tables (FIX-1 through FIX-9) are transcribed accurately. The main correctness gaps are not in the tables but in a few formulas that silently diverge from the addendum: Sun's Cheshta is a flat-30 placeholder where FIX-7 demands Ayana Bala, and the Saptavargaja five-fold friendship map is missing its middle (Sama) rung.

Benefic/malefic classification is centralized in `isNaturalBenefic`, but it is applied inconsistently: Drik Bala passes the combust flag (so a combust Jupiter subtracts) while Bhava Drishti does not (so the same combust Jupiter adds). Both aspect sums also let Rahu and Ketu cast a full 7th-house aspect, because `gradedAspectStrengthBetweenHouses` derives strength purely from house distance and never gates on whether the caster is a node.

Temporal balas are computed from a civil clock, not the Vedic sunrise-anchored day. Weekday-lord balas (Vara 45, Hora 60, Abda 15) read `birthDate.getDay()`, so a birth at 03:00 is attributed to the calendar day rather than the still-running previous Vedic day. Tribhaga leans on a documented half-day (sunset = sunrise + 12h) approximation, and Masa/Abda use documented sign-based proxies.

Relationship geometry (conjunctions, graha/rashi drishti, graha yuddha, parivartana, stelliums, combustion, avastha, gandanta, sandhi, house lordships, upagraha placements) is clean, defensive, and matches REQ-2 including the FIX-10/11/13 decisions. Combustion is the one shared input FIX-7 asked to compute once, yet it is computed independently in both `relationships.ts` and `shadbala.ts`; the values agree because the same function runs twice, so this is duplication rather than a numeric bug. The Jaimini, nakshatra-relationship and sub-lord modules are arithmetically sound and their school-choice approximations are documented inline.

3. **Nodes cast 7th aspect in Drik/Bhava (confirmed)** — `computeDrikBala` and `computeBhavaBala` iterate all planets including Rahu/Ketu, and `gradedAspectStrengthBetweenHouses` gives every body a full 7th aspect, so nodes subtract Drik strength. 7. **Vimsopaka omits exaltation (likely)** — `computeVimsopakaScore` reuses `dignityScoreForVarga`, which drops the exalted row (a FIX-1 anti-double-count rule specific to Saptavargaja). ### Cheshta Bala: Sun's flat 30 overrides FIX-7

`computeCheshtaBala` short-circuits `if (planet === 'Sun') return 30`. FIX-7 is explicit that the Sun's Cheshta is its Ayana Bala and specifically *not* a flat 30, and that Ayana Bala should be the value used. Nothing in the module (or the Kaala Bala assembly, which has no ayana term) computes Ayana Bala, so the Sun receives a constant regardless of its declination/season. This propagates into the Sun's `components.cheshta`, its `total`, `strengthRatio`/`grade`, and — via `computeIshtaKashta(uccha, cheshta)` — its Ishta and Kashta Phala. The Moon path (`return clamp(pakshaBalaValue, 0, 60)`, Paksha in full) and the node path (30) match FIX-7; only the Sun clause contradicts it. The retrograde/direct proxy for the five star planets, the `|speed|/meanMotion` scaling, the 0.5 combustion multiplier, and the 0–60 clamp are all consistent with FIX-7's documented approximation.

### Saptavargaja five-fold map collapses to four levels

FIX-1 names five compound outcomes — Adhimitra 22.5, Mitra 15, Sama 7.5, Shatru 3.75, Adhishatru 1.875 — "determined by COMBINED (permanent + tatkalika) relationship." `combineRelationshipScore` produces only four of them from the compound:

```
perm friend  + temp friend → 22.5 (Adhimitra)
perm friend  + temp enemy  → 15   (Mitra)     ← classical Panchadha maitri: Sama (7.5)
perm neutral + temp friend → 15   (Mitra)
perm neutral + temp enemy  → 3.75 (Shatru)
perm enemy   + temp friend → 3.75 (Shatru)    ← classical Panchadha maitri: Sama (7.5)
perm enemy   + temp enemy  → 1.875 (Adhishatru)
```

`DIGNITY_SAMA` (7.5) is only reachable in the missing-positional-data fallback, never from the compound. This mirrors the older REQ-1.1b table verbatim, so the code is faithful to that table — but the standard BPHS Panchadha maitri routes perm-friend/temp-enemy and perm-enemy/temp-friend to Sama, and FIX-1 lists Sama as a compound rung. The two affected cells shift a planet by ±7.5 virupas per varga (up to ±52.5 across seven vargas), which is astrologically material. This is a spec-internal tension worth resolving with the astrologer rather than a clear code bug.

### Node casters and the combust-benefic split across the two drishti tallies

`gradedAspectStrengthBetweenHouses` computes strength from house distance alone (`3rd/10th=15, 5th/9th=30, 4th/8th=45, 7th=60`, with Jupiter/Mars/Saturn special upgrades to 60). It never checks whether `fromPlanet` is a node, so a Rahu or Ketu sitting 7th from the target contributes a full 60 as a malefic in both `computeDrikBala` and `computeBhavaBala`. Classical Drik Bala restricts casters to the seven grahas; including the nodes systematically depresses Drik Bala for any planet they oppose/aspect.

The benefic/malefic split is where the two consumers diverge. `computeDrikBala` calls:

```ts
isNaturalBenefic(x.planet, { waxingMoon, combust: combustionByPlanet[x.planet]?.combust })
```

so a combust Jupiter/Venus/Mercury is correctly demoted to malefic per FIX-9. `computeBhavaBala` calls:

```ts
isNaturalBenefic(x.planet, { waxingMoon })
```

with no `combust`, so the same combust benefic still adds to `beneficSum`. The waning-Moon demotion is handled in both (both pass `waxingMoon`); only the combustion demotion is missing from Bhava Drishti. The fix is to thread the combustion map (already built once in `computeShadbala`) into `computeBhavaBala`.

### Temporal balas run on a civil clock, not the Vedic day

`computeVaraBala` (45 to the weekday lord), `computeHoraBala` (60 to the birth-hora lord, seeded from the weekday lord) and `computeAbdaBala` (15 to the year lord) all key off `birthDate.getDay()`. The Vedic vara runs from sunrise to the next sunrise, so a 02:30 birth still belongs to the previous weekday until sunrise. As written, such births are attributed to the calendar day, shifting the Vara lord, the Hora seed (`startPos = HORA_SEQUENCE.indexOf(dayLord)`), and potentially the Abda lord. `index.ts` already derives `sunriseSeconds`, so the guard is available: when `birthTimeSeconds < sunriseSeconds`, use the prior day's weekday. The Hora index math itself (`floor(secondsSinceSunrise/3600) % 24`, then `% 7` into the Chaldean-order `HORA_SEQUENCE`) is correct; only its weekday seed inherits the civil-day problem. Note that Tribhaga, which is correctly gated on `sunriseSeconds`/`sunsetSeconds`, does *not* share this bug — its day/night split is right; it only inherits the equal-day-length approximation (issue 9).

### Correct constant tables and edge-case handling worth noting

The transcription of the corrected constants is accurate and worth crediting: FIX-2 Naisargika (60/51.43/42.86/34.29/25.71/17.14/8.57 = 60·n/7), FIX-3 Ojha-Yugma as D1+D9 parity with Moon/Venus preferring even, FIX-4 binary Drekkana with Saturn as neuter, FIX-5 rupa minimums, FIX-6 proportional Natonnata folded to noon/midnight, FIX-8 Vimsopaka weights (6/2/4/5/2/1 = 20), and FIX-9's graded scheme with special-aspect upgrades all match. Exaltation longitudes (Sun 10, Moon 33, Mars 298, Mercury 165, Jupiter 95, Venus 357, Saturn 200) and the D2/D3/D12 sign maps are correct, satisfying REQ-0. Edge cases are handled with real care: `strengthRatio`, `beneficRatio`, the Cheshta mean-motion divisor, and the Tribhaga day/night lengths all guard against division by zero; `normLon`/`arcDist` handle wraparound (including the 0°/360° Pisces-Aries gandanta junction); missing planets are skipped via `find(...) ?? continue`; and the Paksha fold keeps benefic/malefic balas inside 0–60. The relationship module's REQ-2 coverage (including the FIX-10 lower-longitude war winner, FIX-11 dainya>kahala>maha priority, and FIX-13 dual Moon combustion flags) is complete and matches the spec.
