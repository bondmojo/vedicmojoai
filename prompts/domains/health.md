## Domain Knowledge: Health (canonical reference — prompts/domains/health.md)

**Primary houses:** H1/Lagna (vitality, constitution, body), H6 (disease, immunity,
acute illness), H8 (chronic conditions, surgery, longevity), H12 (hospitalisation,
sleep, immune depletion), H3 (courage/vital energy). The lagna lord's strength is the
single most important vitality baseline.

**Karakas:** Sun (soul, vitality, bones, heart), Moon (mind, fluids, emotional health),
Mars (blood, muscles, accidents, surgery), Saturn (chronic disease, nerves, joints,
longevity), Mercury (nervous system, skin), Jupiter (liver, fat metabolism), Venus
(reproductive system, kidneys), Rahu (mysterious/misdiagnosed ailments, toxins),
Ketu (infections, autoimmune, karmic ailments).

**Body mapping (Kalapurusha):** H1 head → H12 feet; afflicted houses point to body
regions. The sign on H6/H8 and planets therein refine the affected system.

**Divisional charts:** D30 (Trimshamsa) is PRIMARY for disease classification — the
D30 sign/lord of the lagna lord and afflicted planets indicate the nature of illness
(Mars portions: inflammation/accidents; Saturn portions: chronic/degenerative;
Mercury: nervous; Jupiter: metabolic; Venus: reproductive/renal).

**Shadbala:** Low total shadbala on the lagna lord or Moon = weak recovery capacity.
A planet with low shadbala ruling H6/H8 gives disease during its periods more easily.

**Key patterns:** Lagna lord in dusthana (6/8/12), Moon afflicted by Saturn/Rahu
(psychological stress), combust lagna lord (depleted vitality), papa-kartari on lagna.
Balarishta-type afflictions matter only if the lagna lord is also weak.

**Dasha activation rules:** Health events cluster in periods of H6/H8/H12 lords,
planets placed in those houses, or a weak/afflicted lagna lord. MD-AD lords in mutual
6/8 = treatment complications or misdiagnosis risk. Retrograde malefic dasha lords
prolong or internalise conditions. Nakshatra lord of the dasha lord afflicted =
hidden layer of the same complaint.

**Transit emphasis:** Sade Sati (esp. peak phase) and Ashtama Shani (Saturn 8th from
Moon) mark endurance-testing windows — read severity with Saturn BAV (≤ 3 = harder).
Saturn/Rahu transits over the lagna lord or H6/H8 lords time onset; Jupiter's transit
over lagna or the lagna lord supports recovery.
