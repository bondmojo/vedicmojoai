## Domain Knowledge: Marriage & Relationships (canonical reference — prompts/domains/marriage.md)

**Primary houses:** H7 (spouse, partnership, marriage), H2 (family growth after
marriage), H11 (fulfilment of desire), H4 (domestic happiness), H12 (bed comforts,
marital intimacy), H8 (marital longevity, in-laws, sudden ruptures).

**Karakas:** Venus (marriage karaka for all charts; spouse for male charts), Jupiter
(husband karaka in female charts), Moon (mind and emotional bonding), Mars
(passion — and Kuja Dosha when placed in 1/2/4/7/8/12 from lagna, Moon, or Venus,
unless cancelled by own/exalted sign, benefic aspect, or matching dosha in partner).

**Divisional charts:** D9 (Navamsa) is PRIMARY for marriage — the D9 lagna lord,
planets in D9 H7, the D9 dignity of Venus/Jupiter, and vargottama status of the H7
lord show marriage quality and durability. A D1 H7 promise only delivers if D9
confirms it.

**Jaimini:** Darakaraka (DK — lowest degree planet) is the spouse significator; its
sign, house, and dignity describe the spouse and the timing of union (DK dasha/AD
periods). Upapada Lagna (UL) and its 2nd house show marriage stability; malefics in
2nd from UL strain the marriage.

**Key patterns:** H7 lord in dusthana (6/8/12) or combust = friction/delay; Saturn's
aspect on H7/Venus delays but stabilises after ~28; Rahu in H7 gives unconventional
or cross-cultural unions; afflicted Venus (combust, debilitated, hemmed) = romantic
disillusionment cycles.

**Dasha activation rules:** Marriage and relationship events fire in periods of the
H7 lord (D1 or D9), Venus/Jupiter (as karaka), DK, planets in H7, or the UL lord.
MD-AD in 6/8 mutual placement = relational friction phases. H8-lord periods test
in-law relations and marital endurance; H12-lord periods emphasise intimacy or
separation-by-distance.

**Transit emphasis:** Jupiter transiting H7 or over the H7 lord / Venus opens
commitment windows; Saturn transiting H7 stresses bonds (worse with BAV ≤ 3);
Rahu-Ketu axis across 1-7 marks karmic relationship turning points; Sade Sati
often coincides with domestic redefinition.
