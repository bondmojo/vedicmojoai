## Domain Knowledge: Career (canonical reference — prompts/domains/career.md)

**Primary houses:** H10 (karma, profession, status), H6 (service, employment, daily work),
H2 (earned income), H11 (gains, promotions, network), H7 (business partnerships, public dealings).
H8 transits/dashas over H10 factors = career upheaval or research/occult professions.

**Karakas:** Sun (authority, government, leadership), Saturn (service, labour, discipline,
longevity of career), Mercury (commerce, communication, analysis), Jupiter (advisory,
teaching, law, finance), Mars (engineering, defence, surgery, real estate execution).

**Divisional charts:** D10 (Dasamsa) is PRIMARY for career — D10 lagna lord, D10 10th
lord, and planets in D10 kendras define the professional field and stature. D9 (Navamsa)
shows the inner strength/fortune backing the career; a planet strong in D1 but weak in
D9 gives status without durability (and vice versa — vargottama planets are especially
reliable).

**Jaimini:** Amatyakaraka (AK2 — second-highest degree) is the career significator;
its dasha periods and house placement time professional rise. Arudha Lagna (AL) and
its 10th show perceived status.

**Key yogas:** Dharma-Karmadhipati (H9-H10 lord combination) = career peak yoga.
Pancha Mahapurusha yogas (Ruchaka/Bhadra/Hamsa/Malavya/Shasha) shape the professional
archetype. Viparita Raja Yoga (6/8/12 lords in mutual dusthanas) gives sudden rises
after others' losses.

**Dasha activation rules:** Periods of the 10th lord (D1 and D10), Amatyakaraka,
planets in H10, or planets aspecting the 10th activate career events. A dasha lord
placed in H6 gives service/job changes; in H12, foreign work or behind-the-scenes
roles. MD lord related to AD lord by kendra/trikona from each other = smooth delivery;
in 6/8 (shashtashtaka) = friction between agendas.

**Transit emphasis:** Saturn's transit over natal H10/H10 lord or the D10 lagna lord
times visible career restructuring. Jupiter's transit over H10/H11 or the dasha lord
supports promotion windows. Sade Sati phases often coincide with role redefinition
rather than pure loss — read with BAV scores.
