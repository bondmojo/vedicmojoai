## Domain Knowledge: Cashflow / Money (canonical reference — prompts/domains/cashflow.md)

Cashflow is LIQUIDITY — the month-to-month balance of income, expenses, and debt.
It is distinct from Wealth (long-term accumulation): a chart can show strong
accumulation with chronically tight liquidity, or easy cash with nothing retained.

**Primary houses:** H11 (active income streams, receivables), H2 (cash reserves),
H12 (expenditure, outflows, EMIs paid), H6 (debt, loans taken, service income),
H8 (others' money — credit, insurance, dues), H3 (self-effort income, side work).

**Karakas:** Moon (liquidity itself — a strong unafflicted Moon smooths cash cycles),
Mercury (transactions, trading turnover), Jupiter (surplus), Saturn (delayed
receivables, structural expenses), Rahu (leverage, borrowed money).

**Divisional charts:** D2 (Hora) — read the BALANCE: planets in Moon hora support
circulating cash; Sun hora supports earning power that must be actively exerted.

**Key ratios to assess:** relative strength of H11 lord vs H12 lord (income vs
outflow); H6 lord strength (debt-service capacity — a strong H6 lord manages debt,
a weak afflicted one drowns in it); Moon's dignity and aspects (liquidity stress
when Saturn/Rahu afflict the Moon).

**Key patterns:** H11 lord in H12 = income leaks as fast as it arrives. H12 lord in
H2 = reserves fund recurring expenses. H2-H11 lords exchange = self-reinforcing cash
cycle. H6 lord with Rahu = compounding leverage. Kemadruma-type isolated Moon =
erratic cash rhythm even when net worth is fine.

**Dasha activation rules:** Cash-tight windows fire in periods of H12/H6 lords or
planets in H12; receivable windows in H11/H2 lord periods and Moon periods (if the
Moon is strong). MD of an income lord with AD of an expense lord = high-turnover,
low-retention phases. Combust or debilitated dasha lords delay payments due.

**Transit emphasis:** Saturn transiting H2/H11 slows receivables and forces expense
discipline; Jupiter over H11/H2 opens inflow windows (BAV ≥ 4 confirms); Rahu over
H6/H8 tempts leverage spirals; Sade Sati commonly coincides with restructured
obligations (loans, EMIs, dependents) rather than outright loss.
