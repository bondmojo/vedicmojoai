## Domain Knowledge: Wealth (canonical reference — prompts/domains/wealth.md)

**Primary houses:** H2 (accumulated wealth, family assets, speech), H11 (gains, income
streams, fulfilment of desires), H9 (fortune, windfalls), H5 (speculation, investments,
purva-punya), H8 (inheritance, insurance, others' money), H12 (expenditure, losses,
donations).

**Karakas:** Jupiter (wealth karaka — expansion, prosperity), Venus (luxury, comforts,
liquid wealth), Mercury (trade, accounting), Moon (cash flow, liquidity when strong).

**Divisional charts:** D2 (Hora) is PRIMARY for wealth capacity — planets in the Sun's
hora indicate wealth through self-effort/authority; in the Moon's hora, through
liquidity, family, and public dealings. Benefics in own hora strengthen accumulation.

**Jaimini:** Check Arudha Lagna (AL) — planets in 2nd/11th from AL give tangible
wealth; benefics in 12th from AL drain it. The Darapada (A7) and its relation to AL
indicate wealth through partnerships/market.

**Key yogas:** Dhana yogas — combinations of the 1st/2nd/5th/9th/11th lords by
conjunction, aspect, or exchange (e.g. 2nd lord in 11th, 11th lord in 2nd, 5th-9th
exchange). Lakshmi Yoga (strong 9th lord + Venus dignity). Daridra (poverty) markers:
2nd/11th lords in 6/8/12, combust, or debilitated without cancellation. Viparita
(especially Vipareeta from H8 lord) can give sudden inheritance-type gains.

**Dasha activation rules:** Wealth materialises in periods of Dhana-yoga participants,
the 2nd/11th lords, or planets placed in H2/H11. A dasha lord in H12 or lord of H12
raises expenditure even when income holds. Debilitated dasha lords with active
Neechabhanga can flip from loss to sudden recovery mid-period. MD-AD pairs that
jointly complete a Dhana yoga (one lord each) are the strongest accumulation windows.

**Transit emphasis:** Jupiter transiting H2/H11 or over the natal 2nd/11th lords marks
gain windows (check Jupiter BAV ≥ 4). Saturn transiting H2 disciplines or constricts
cash; over H11 it slows but consolidates gains. Rahu transits over H2/H11 give
volatile, speculative swings.
