## Domain Knowledge: Property & Assets (canonical reference — prompts/domains/property.md)

**Primary houses:** H4 (land, home, vehicles, fixed assets, mother), H2 (accumulated
assets), H11 (gains enabling acquisition), H8 (inherited property, joint assets),
H12 (property loss, distant/foreign property), H3 (documents, agreements, moves).

**Karakas:** Mars (land, construction, real estate), Venus (vehicles, luxury property,
interiors), Moon (home comfort, water-adjacent property), Saturn (old properties,
long-term holdings, land disputes), Ketu (ancestral property).

**Divisional charts:** D4 (Chaturthamsa) is PRIMARY for fixed assets — the D4 lagna
lord and D4 4th house/lord show acquisition capacity and stability of holdings;
malefics in D4 kendras without dignity indicate disputes or frequent relocation.

**Key patterns:** Strong H4 lord + Mars well-placed = property acquisition promise.
H4 lord in H12 = property far from birthplace or liquidation. Saturn-Mars affliction
on H4 = construction delays or litigation. Rahu in H4 = leveraged (loan-heavy)
acquisition. H4 lord with H8 lord = inheritance route.

**Dasha activation rules:** Property events fire in periods of the H4 lord, Mars
(as karaka), planets in H4, or the D4 lagna lord. An MD-AD pair linking H4 and H11
lords = purchase window; linking H4 and H12 = sale/relocation; with H6 involvement =
loan-financed transactions or disputes. Saturn sub-periods favour land consolidation;
Venus sub-periods favour homes and vehicles.

**Transit emphasis:** Jupiter transiting H4 or over the H4 lord supports acquisition
(BAV ≥ 4 confirms); Saturn transiting H4 brings relocation pressure, renovation, or
disputes; Kantaka Shani phases obstruct mobility and property momentum; Rahu over H4
accelerates leveraged deals but with volatility.
